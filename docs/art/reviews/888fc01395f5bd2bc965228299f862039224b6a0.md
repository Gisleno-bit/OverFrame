## Changed behavior

A surviving frontal block now retains coverage during its ordinary shieldstun, for both melee and projectiles. A broken shield remains vulnerable even when regeneration has restored a small positive health value. The distinction is recorded at both break sites, damage and holding decay, and included in the rollback checksum.

Serving shieldstun returns to `Shield` with a reset frame counter. Previously that granted a free powershield on the completion tick without a new shield raise. The new arming flag distinguishes a real raise from that recovery, preserves the existing powershield window, and is covered by the checksum. No attack tables, movement timings, health regeneration, out-of-shield options, animation, hitbox geometry or character dimensions changed.
